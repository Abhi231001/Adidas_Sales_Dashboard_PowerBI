The Adidas Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales, profit, products, regions, states, gender categories, and sales methods.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of overall business performance through KPIs, trend analysis, product-level analysis, geographic analysis, and interactive filters. It helps identify high-performing products and regions, understand monthly sales trends, and compare different sales channels.

# Objectives

Monitor overall sales and units sold.

Analyze monthly sales trends.

Identify profitable products.

Compare sales performance by gender.

Analyze regional and state-level performance.

Compare different sales methods.

Support data-driven business decisions.

# 1. Monthly Sales Trend

Insight:

Sales fluctuate throughout the year.

July has the highest monthly sales at approximately 9.5M.

August remains strong at around 9.2M.

March records the lowest sales at approximately 5.7M.

Sales recover toward the end of the year, reaching around 8.5M in December.

Business Solution :

Increase inventory before high-sales months.

Launch promotions during low-sales periods.

Plan marketing campaigns around seasonal demand.

Allocate staff and resources based on expected sales volume.

Business action: Increase promotional activities around low-performing months such as March and prepare inventory ahead of peak periods such as July and August.

# 2. Sales by Gender / Product Category

Insight:

The treemap shows sales distributed across men's and women's product categories.

Men's Apparel – 12M

Women's Athletic Footwear – 11M

Other men's and women's footwear/apparel categories also contribute significantly.

Business Solution:

Maintain strong inventory for high-performing categories.

Develop new products around popular categories.

Create targeted marketing campaigns for men's and women's segments.

Analyze lower-performing categories and improve their positioning.

Business action: Focus product development and marketing on categories generating the highest sales while investigating why lower-performing categories have weaker demand.

# 3. Profit by Sales Method

Insight:

In-store has the largest share, while online contributes a smaller but significant portion.

Business Solution:

Improve the online shopping experience.

Invest in digital marketing.

Use promotions to increase online sales.

Optimize inventory across stores and outlets.

Compare profitability across channels before allocating marketing budgets.

Business action: Strengthen online sales while continuing to optimize the strong in-store channel.

# 4. Profit by Product

Insight:

Men's Street Footwear is the strongest-performing product category in this chart.

Business Solution:

Increase stock availability for high-performing products.

Give more marketing attention to profitable categories.

# 5. Sales by State

Insight:

The map provides a geographic view of sales performance across the United States. Darker-shaded states represent relatively higher sales compared with lighter-shaded states.

This helps identify high-performing and lower-performing geographic markets.

Business Solution:

Identify high-demand markets.

Allocate inventory based on geographic demand.

Target regional advertising campaigns.

Expand operations in promising markets.

Investigate weak-performing states.

Business action: Concentrate inventory and marketing resources in high-performing states while using targeted promotions to improve weaker markets.

# 6. Profit by Region

Insight:

The West is the largest contributor, while the Midwest has the smallest share among the regions shown.

Business Solution:

Maintain strong operations in the West.

Identify factors driving the West's strong performance.

Increase marketing efforts in lower-performing regions.

Develop region-specific pricing and promotional strategies.
